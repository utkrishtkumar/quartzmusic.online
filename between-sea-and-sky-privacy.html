Between Sea and Sky – Privacy Policy

Developer: KenshiLabs · Effective 11 November 2026

This policy covers the Android game Between Sea and Sky (package com.kenshilabs.betweenseaandsky). In short: the game does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. We (the developer) do not receive any information about you or your device from the game.

Data kept on your device

The game saves your settings on your device so they are there next time: graphics quality, controls, driving assists, units and the car you chose. This stays on your device, is never sent to us or anyone else, and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Internet use: the in-game radio

The game itself runs entirely from your device and works offline. The only part that goes online is the optional in-game radio. When you switch a station on, your device plays that station's live stream straight from the broadcaster's server:

  • Capital, Heart, Radio X, Capital Dance, Capital XTRA, Smooth, Classic FM and Gold, from Global Media & Entertainment (musicradio.com)
  • Radio Paradise (radioparadise.com)
  • SomaFM Groove Salad (somafm.com)

As with any internet connection, the broadcaster's server receives your IP address and basic technical details (such as the type of device and app making the request) so it can deliver the audio. That data goes directly to the broadcaster under its own privacy policy; we never receive or see it. When the radio is off, the game makes no internet connections.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access only, which is used for the radio streams described above.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
